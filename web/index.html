Linked Lamps

Direct Bluetooth control over Meshtastic's own GATT link - no phone app, no need to leave the Meshtastic firmware. Injects a lamp packet on the private app port, exactly as a tap on the lamp itself would.
First connection: your OS will show a native Bluetooth pairing prompt - enter 123456 (the lamps' fixed BLE PIN). Chrome/Edge only; Safari and Firefox do not implement Web Bluetooth.
